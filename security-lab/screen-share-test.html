Check what a real browser receives from the Linux screen-capture portal. This page does not upload, transmit, or automatically save your screen. You explicitly choose when to share and when to download evidence.

Controlled A/B verification

  1. Run python3 security-lab/overlay_probe.py and verify the three-color marker is visible on your monitor.
  2. With exclusion off, click Share screen and select the entire monitor containing the marker.
  3. Capture the baseline. It must detect the color marker before any exclusion claim is meaningful.
  4. Enable a supported compositor rule, keeping the same monitor and live share; verify the marker remains visible to you locally.
  5. Capture the after frame. Review both images and export the local report.
Choose the entire monitor in the sharing dialog to begin.

Stock GNOME whole-monitor capture includes the overlay. The custom Mutter filter requires a compatible deployed runtime, explicit activation, and a full desktop logout/login. Check that the app reports runtime active before testing it. A login restart ends the old share; frames from different sessions are not a same-stream comparison.
